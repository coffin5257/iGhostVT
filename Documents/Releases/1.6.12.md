iGhostVT 1.6.12 clears the Live Activity when quitting ends your shells.

- With Background Activities off, quitting the app ended its shells but left the Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, still listing terminals that no longer existed. The quit now ends the activity along with the shells. An app iOS has suspended in the background gets no notice when it is swiped away, so its shells and its activity stay, as before.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.12-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.12.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
