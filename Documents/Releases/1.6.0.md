iGhostVT 1.6.0 brings remote terminals back faster and speeds up what a host sends over a slow connection, and starts a new release line: it connects only to devices on 1.6.

- Coming back to a terminal on another device — opening the app, returning to it, or the network coming back — waited for the host to accept the connection before asking for the terminal, and then for up to 256 KB of its recent output. The device now asks for the terminal together with the connection, and the host compresses what it sends, so the screen fills in about half the time: on a Wi‑Fi link with 30 ms round trips a reconnect took 222–265 ms and now takes 124–145 ms, on a cellular link about one second and now about half a second, and on a very poor link six seconds and now two.
- Text-heavy output from a host — a long log, a build, an `sz` download of a text file — took a slow link's full bandwidth. It is now compressed on the way, and an 8 MB text file comes down 1.7 to 2.3 times as fast; files that are already compressed transfer as before. What you type is sent as it was.
- Devices on different releases cannot connect to each other. Update every device to 1.6.0 together: iGhostVT on your Macs and jailbroken devices, and Ghost Remote on your other iPhones and iPads.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.0-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.0.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
