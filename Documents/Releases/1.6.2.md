iGhostVT 1.6.2 keeps the tab count on iPhone whole.

- On iPhone with iOS 26, the number of open tabs on the bottom bar's tab button was cut off at the button's edge whenever the button was pressed or the title capsule beside it flowed into it. The count now sits on top of the button and stays whole.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.2-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.2.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
