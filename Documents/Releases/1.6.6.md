iGhostVT 1.6.6 lets `ighostvt-cli` work on the devices the app is paired with, and keeps every session when the app quits.

- `ighostvt-cli` could only reach the device it ran on. `--host <host>` (or `IGHOSTVT_HOST`) now runs `list`, `capture`, `send`, `new` and `kill` on a paired device, nearby or through the relay. It uses iGhostVT's own pairings, so every device the app is paired with works straight away, nicknames included. `ighostvt-cli hosts` lists them, `hosts --nearby` lists the ones it can reach, and `ighostvt-cli pair <host>` pairs with a new one, which then shows up in iGhostVT without a relaunch.
- Quitting iGhostVT with Keep Sessions Running off ended every shell. The switch is gone: a quit now keeps every session for the next launch, and closing a tab or `ighostvt-cli kill` ends one.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.6-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.6.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
