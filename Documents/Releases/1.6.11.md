iGhostVT 1.6.11 keeps your terminals running whenever Remote Access is on.

- With Remote Access on, turning off Background Activities meant quitting the app ended the shells a paired device was using or about to come back to. While Remote Access is on, Background Activities now stays on and its switch is greyed out, with a note saying why; turn Remote Access off and your own choice applies again.
- In Chinese, the Accessory Keys row repeated 键盘 under a Keyboard section that already says it. It is now 辅助键.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.11-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.11.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
