iGhostVT 1.6.4 tidies Remote Access settings and puts new tabs where you expect them.

- A new tab for another directory or another device's terminal landed right after the active tab and pushed the rest along. Only a tab that copies the active one — ⌘T, or the menu row for the active tab's own device and directory — goes beside it now; every other new tab goes at the end.
- On a Mac, the buttons in the Remote Access device tables showed only on the selected row, so the details beside them shifted as the selection moved, and a paired device could not be renamed. Every row keeps its buttons, the details line up, Check for Update has its own icon, and the pencil renames a paired device.
- A device that had just come online or changed its name could take a while to appear in Remote Access. A refresh button beside the Authorized Devices table on the Mac, and pulling down the page on iPhone and iPad, ask the helper, the local network and the relay again at once.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.4-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.4.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
