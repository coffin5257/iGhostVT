iGhostVT 1.6.10 brings back the switch that ends your shells when the app quits, and puts the settings page back into four short sections.

- Every shell kept running after you quit the app, with no way to have quitting end them. Settings ▸ Sessions ▸ Background Activities (General on the Mac) is that switch again, on by default. Turned off, quitting ends the shells your tabs run on this device; terminals on a paired device, and ones a paired device opened here, keep running. Ghost Remote has no shells of its own, so it has no such switch.
- The settings page had grown a section for almost every switch, each with its own note. It is now Display, Sessions, Keyboard and About, with a note only where one is needed. In iGhostVT, Remote Access sits under Recent Directories and Background Activities; in Ghost Remote it is at the top of Sessions, where you see it without scrolling. Accessory Keys comes before Shortcuts, and New Session at Launch has moved to About ▸ Advanced.
- Settings ▸ Remote Access opened on a bare name field with a note under it. The name now has its own row beside Allow Remote Access, and tapping it while it is empty fills in the device's name to edit; Accessible Devices no longer shows as an empty heading when nothing is paired.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.10-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.10.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
