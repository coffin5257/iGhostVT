iGhostVT 1.6.8 stops its widget from crashing on iOS 15, and tidies the settings page.

- On iOS 15 the widget extension crashed every time the system started it, because it needed ActivityKit, which arrived in iOS 16.1. Crash reports piled up even though the app itself worked. The extension now runs on iOS 15 and simply offers no Live Activity there. On iOS 16.2 and later the Live Activity is unchanged.
- Settings are split into clearer sections in a new order, Remote Access and Advanced Options each open on a page of their own, and on an iPhone a theme's subtitle sits under its name instead of beside it.
- Opening and leaving the license list no longer makes its header jump.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.8-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.8.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
