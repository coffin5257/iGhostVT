iGhostVT 1.6.20 lets the app reach the network on an iPhone or iPad sold in China, and keeps the tab overview button working after the ⋯ menu.

- On an iPhone or iPad sold in China, iGhostVT could fail to connect to a paired device, directly or through the relay, as if there were no network. iOS keeps a WLAN & Cellular setting for each app and never asks about one a package manager installed, so it could stay off. iGhostVT now turns on Wi-Fi and cellular data for itself when it opens. Shells, remote access to this device and `ighostvt-cli` were never affected.
- Tapping ⋯ and then the tab overview button quickly could leave that button doing nothing until iGhostVT was restarted. It now opens the overview every time.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.20-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.20.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
