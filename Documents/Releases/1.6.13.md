iGhostVT 1.6.13 says what each paired device is doing in the new-tab menu, adds Copy as One Line, and keeps the selection highlight on the text it covers.

- In the new-tab menu, a paired device that was slow to answer, or out of reach, used to drop out of the list or show an empty submenu, and its terminals could vanish until the next poll. Every paired device is listed now. One that cannot be reached is greyed out as Not Reachable. One that is reachable but has not answered shows Connecting… or Not Responding where its terminals would be. A device's terminals stay listed while it is briefly out of sight, and the menu waits a little longer for a device it has not heard from yet.
- Open All was offered only when a device held several terminals, and not always in the same place. It now sits right under the device's New Terminal whenever the device holds at least one terminal. It is greyed out once all of them are open here, and gone when the device holds none.
- Copying a selection that spans several rows always kept the line breaks, so a command wrapped across rows pasted as several lines. Copy as One Line in the selection menu trims each row and joins the rows with nothing between them.
- When a program redrew its screen in place under a touch selection, the highlight kept the shape of the text that was there before. It now follows the text under it.
