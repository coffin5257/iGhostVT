iGhostVT 1.6.3 shows how far a Mac's update has downloaded.

- On a Mac, Check for Updates… sat on "Downloading iGhostVT" with an empty progress bar until the download was done, and `ighostvt-cli update` printed nothing new while it ran: every progress report from the terminal helper was dropped on its way to the app. The bar and the percentage now follow the download. A Mac updates to 1.6.3 with the old helper, so this update still shows the empty bar once; the ones after it show progress.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.3-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.3.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
