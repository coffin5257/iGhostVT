iGhostVT 1.6.9 keeps the terminal in place on iOS 15 iPhones when the keyboard comes up.

- On an iPhone running iOS 15, typing with the keyboard up could push the whole terminal up by the height of the status bar, so its first line sat under the clock. With the keyboard down again, the bar stayed raised and the terminal used only half the screen. The ⋯ button in the bottom bar was growing to fill the space beside the keyboard; it now keeps its size. iPads and iOS 16 and later are unchanged.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.9-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.9.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
